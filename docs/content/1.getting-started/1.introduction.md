The Aviation Herald Scraper reads the public listing pages on [avherald.com](https://avherald.com/) and saves the headline line from each row.

You get the link, the headline, the date printed above the row, and the category icon (crash, accident, incident, news, or report). You do not get the article. The program never opens the report page.

## What you can do

- **Keep a personal list** of headlines you have already seen.
- **Continue later.** A second run starts at the saved list page.
- **Filter** by category and by whether the site sorts on last update or occurrence date.
- **Print a few rows** in the terminal when you only want three or four headlines.
- **Export** the same rows as JSON, CSV, or Excel.

## What it will not do

- Download or store article bodies.
- Guess article ids or page numbers. It follows the site's Next button.
- Redistribute the site's text, or use it to train a model.
- Hide its identity. The request says it is this tool.

The site has no public API. The [Impressum](https://avherald.com/h?impressum=) and the copyright line on every page reserve all rights and prohibit reproduction, redistribution, and AI use. Read [Legal](/project/legal) before you run a large crawl.
