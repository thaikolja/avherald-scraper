The Aviation Herald Scraper is not affiliated with The Aviation Herald.

The [Impressum](https://avherald.com/h?impressum=) names the publisher as NOMIS SOFT Datenverarbeitung GmbH, Salzburg. The copyright line on that page, and on the rest of the site, says:

> © 2008-2026 by The Aviation Herald, all rights reserved, reproduction, redistribution and AI learning/use prohibited.

`robots.txt` disallows `/` for every user agent.

## Why the program exists

The repository ships original code. It does not contain the site's articles. A person who runs a small, delayed crawl and keeps only listing headlines on their own computer can argue fair use under [17 U.S.C. § 107](https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/17/107):

- The purpose is a personal or educational reference, not a competing publication.
- The headings are short facts, not the creative report.
- The amount stops at the headline, the date, the category, and the link.
- That copy does not replace the article, and it is not used to train a model.

Fair use is a defense, decided one case at a time. It is not permission from the publisher. It does not cover bulk republication or AI training. The publisher is Austrian. US fair use is not a rule the site adopted.

## How the tool stays inside that limit

- It requests list pages only.
- It waits between those requests. The default pause is 3 seconds.
- It sends a User-Agent that names the tool.
- It has no feature for republishing the archive or for model training.

Keep crawls small.
